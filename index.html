        A journey of creativity and code

        ✨ Hi, I'm Mónica!

        I grew up loving games like Mega Man X, Kirby: The Crystal Shards, Yoshi's Story, Ninja Gaiden, and always wondered how they were made. But I didn't think game dev was possible for me, so I tried other paths: Mechanical Engineering, call centers, even manicure (I loved doing anime nail art!). A Java backend bootcamp got me into coding, but it wasn't until I joined a Unity game dev bootcamp that everything clicked.

        Now I'm learning fast through jams and team projects—working on player movement, enemy AI, damage logic, and UI. I'm still not the fastest programmer, but I'm improving every day. My goal is to make unique games that feel as memorable as the classics I grew up with.

        Outside of code, I draw, dance, box, and have seven cats. I love nature documentaries and anything creative.

        I'm still growing, but I'm finally on the right track.
